    How agents use it

    HTTP + x402

    POST /v1/check answers 402 with the price; the agent pays and gets the verdict in the same request.

    What it reads

    Approvals, raw transactions, and EIP-712 signatures: Permit, Permit2, Seaport and x402 payments (EIP-3009). It checks who gets access and the token itself: honeypots, fake look-alikes and high taxes.

    Token verdict

    GET /v1/token?chain=solana&address=…, $0.01 on Base or Solana: before buying or accepting a token, a grade (SAFE to AVOID), reasons like active mint or freeze authority, unlocked LP, thin liquidity or a few big holders, and market data. Solana, Base, Ethereum and more.

    Approval audit

    GET /v1/approvals?chain=base&address=…, $0.02 on Base or Solana: every open token approval of a wallet, who the spender is (flagged, a plain wallet, unverified, unlimited), and which ones to revoke. Base, Ethereum and more.

    OpenAPI, /.well-known/x402 and the CDP Bazaar.
